# Title Generator Prompt

Generate Chinese platform title candidates for the rewritten script.

Prefer title angles like:
- 为什么火
- 海外 AI 工具观察
- 开发者为什么关注这个 CLI
- AI 商业机会拆解
- 一个开源项目/新产品，中文用户该怎么看

Avoid:
- exaggerated income promises
- "照做赚钱" / "教你赚钱" framing
- medical, legal, or financial advice framing
- titles that imply unverified facts

Return 3-8 title options.
