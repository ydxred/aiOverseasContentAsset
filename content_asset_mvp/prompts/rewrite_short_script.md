# Rewrite Short Script Prompt

Rewrite the source into a Chinese narrative short-video asset.

Requirements:
- Do not translate sentence by sentence.
- Do not reuse the original narrative structure mechanically.
- Add context useful for Chinese audiences.
- Mark uncertain claims with 待核查.
- Avoid relying on original video footage.
- Focus on overseas AI opportunity observation, AI tool/CLI/open-source project explanation, and business model teardown.
- Do not promise income, exaggerate certainty, provide gray-market paths, or present copying the source as a route to results.

Return markdown sections:
- 标题
- 口播稿
  - 为什么突然值得关注
  - 海外发生了什么
  - 它解决什么问题
  - 对中文用户/开发者/创作者/创业者的启发
  - 边界：不承诺收益、不夸大、不照搬
- 分镜建议
- 屏幕文字
- 风险点
- 待核查内容
